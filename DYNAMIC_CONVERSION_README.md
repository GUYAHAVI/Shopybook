## How It Works

### 1. **Setup Conversion Rules**
First, create conversion rules for your products:
- **Product**: Greenhouse Film
- **Purchase Unit**: kg
- **Sale Unit**: sqm
- **Conversion Factor**: 0.2 (microns)
- **Purchase Cost**: KSh 100 per kg
- **Sale Price**: KSh 500 per sqm

### 2. **Dynamic Selling Process**
When a customer comes in:

1. **Select the product** you want to sell
2. **Enter the quantity** they want to buy
3. **Choose the unit** they prefer (kg or sqm)
4. **Get instant calculations**:
   - Converted quantity
   - Profit margin
   - Suggested pricing
   - Financial breakdown

### 3. **Example Scenarios**

#### Scenario 1: Customer wants 50 sqm
- You have 4000kg in stock
- System calculates: 50 sqm × 0.2 microns = 10 kg needed
- Purchase cost: 10 kg × KSh 100 = KSh 1,000
- Sale value: 50 sqm × KSh 500 = KSh 25,000
- Profit: KSh 24,000 (96% margin)

#### Scenario 2: Customer wants 100 kg
- You have 4000kg in stock
- System calculates: 100 kg ÷ 0.2 microns = 500 sqm equivalent
- Purchase cost: 100 kg × KSh 100 = KSh 10,000
- Sale value: 100 kg × KSh 200 = KSh 20,000
- Profit: KSh 10,000 (50% margin)

## Technical Details

### Conversion Formulas
- **Weight to Area**: `quantity ÷ micron_thickness = area`
- **Area to Weight**: `quantity × micron_thickness = weight`
- **Custom**: `quantity × conversion_factor = result`

### Supported Units
- **Weight**: kg, g, lbs
- **Area**: sqm, sqft, acres
- **Custom**: Any unit combination

### Micron Presets
- Greenhouse Film: 0.2 microns
- Dam Liners: 0.3, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 microns
